This project explores a repeatable framework for combining mission planning, onboard sensing, and rapid data review for next-generation aviation systems. The team is focused on safe, transparent experimentation with a strong emphasis on human oversight and traceable results.

## Focus areas

- Rapid prototyping of guidance logic for unmanned aircraft concepts
- Integration of multi-source sensing for situational awareness
- Structured post-flight analysis and reporting for engineering teams
