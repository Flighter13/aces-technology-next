## Introduction

In this project, we provide an informative visualization of the complex network that is modern combat battlespace for United States operatives. The focus is on new technologies, atmospheric and platform interactions, and emerging capabilities.

## Format

The data will be presented as an online, three-dimensional, interactive visualization, with supporting profile cards for individual objects. If possible, a physical depiction of some models will be created for display at the ACES Tech ALPHA Conference.

## Invitation to the Defense Industry

The project seeks accurate, unclassified information and encourages defense industry members and military contractors to contribute data. Three-dimensional models, design details, and verified descriptions are especially valuable when they preserve intellectual property and security.

## Content: What to Include

- The future of combat aircraft: NGAD, Skyborg, F/A-XX, Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA)
- Specific scenes representing current and near-future conflicts: Ukraine and Russia, Israel and Hamas, China over the South China Sea
- Artificial intelligence programs such as X-62A VISTA and the F-16 VENOM project
- Private company designs including Heron's Chimera engine and Quarterhorse concepts

## Objects and Entities

- Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA)
- Link 16 and advanced datalinks
- Next Generation Air Dominance (NGAD) fighters
- U.S. Navy F/A-XX platforms
- Low-cost platforms
- Quarterhorse Mk 0 and Mk 1

Though not combat aircraft themselves, some platforms are fundamental to the next wave of systems, especially as enablers for subsonic, supersonic, and hypersonic mission concepts.
