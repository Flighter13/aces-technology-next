## Introduction

The NexGen Air Battlespace project provides an informative visualization of the interconnected systems that define modern U.S. air combat operations. The focus is on emerging technologies, operational concepts, and platform-to-platform interaction across the broader mission environment.

*Note: Any non-factual or speculative commentary in this project reflects team opinion and is not an official statement from any organization.*

## Format

Project outputs are organized as a strategic two-dimensional visualization with supporting profile cards for individual platforms and systems. If feasible, selected concepts may also be represented in physical model form for demonstrations.

## Content: What to Include

Priority inclusion areas include:

* Future combat aircraft ecosystems: Next-Generation Air Dominance (NGAD), the Skyborg program, F/A-XX, and Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA)
* Strategic air conflict contexts and emerging threat environments
* Artificial intelligence in combat aviation, including X-62A VISTA and the F-16 VENOM initiative
* Innovative private-sector developments, including Chimera engine concepts and Quarterhorse aircraft variants

## Objects and Entities

The project map and associated profiles are centered on systems such as:

* Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA)

  * Increment 1: YFQ-42A Dark Merlin and YFQ-44A Fury
* Link 16 and related tactical datalink architectures
* Autonomous refueling platforms (for example, X-47 and MQ-25)
* Low-Cost Attritable Aircraft Technology pathways (for example, XQ-58A)
* Next-generation refueling support concepts
* Quarterhorse Mk 0 and Mk 1 technology demonstrators

Some listed platforms are not direct combat aircraft but remain critical enablers for subsonic, supersonic, and hypersonic transition pathways in future mission architectures.
