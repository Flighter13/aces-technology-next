## Combat Aircraft Technology and New Developments

### Introduction

Advanced fighter aircraft technology has seen major advances in recent years. This conference introduces the systems, programs, and design trends shaping the future of modern aerial combat.

### Major Projects and Aircraft

Several major efforts define the current generation of air combat capabilities:

#### The JSF/IST Program

The Joint Strike Fighter (JSF) and Integrated Systems Technology (IST) efforts established the technical foundation for modern multirole platforms and integrated mission systems.

#### NexGen Air Battlespace

This project explores how modern and emerging platforms operate as a connected ecosystem rather than as isolated aircraft programs.

#### Notable Aircraft

- F-15EX Strike Eagle
- F-16 Fighting Falcon (Viper) and variants
- F-22 Raptor
- F-35 Lightning II
- Su-57 Felon
- Shenyang FC-31 Gyrfalcon
- KAI KF-21 Boramae

### Emerging Technologies

The conference surveys priority technology vectors for next-generation air operations:

- Next-Generation Air Dominance (NGAD) aircraft
- Collaborative Combat Aircraft (CCA)
- Advanced Electro-Optical Targeting System (EOTS)
- Sensor and data fusion
- Aircraft datalink systems
- Artificial intelligence co-pilot concepts
- Directed-energy weapons (for example, laser systems)
- Microelectromechanical systems (MEMS)

### JAST/IST and JSF F-35 Program

The Joint Advanced Strike Technology (JAST)/IST pathway converged into the F-35 program through competitive development and systems integration.

Key themes include:

- Cross-contractor collaborative competition
- Integration of advanced avionics and mission systems
- Incorporation of pre-existing design work where practical
- Program-defining capabilities:
  - Electro-Optical Targeting Systems (EOTS)
  - LiftFan and V/STOL configuration
  - Very-Low Observable (VLO) profile
  - Electro-hydrostatic actuators

### Conclusion

Combat aircraft evolution continues to accelerate through program-level collaboration, modular system design, and rapid technology maturation. The outlook emphasizes integration, autonomy, and survivability as central themes for future air superiority.
