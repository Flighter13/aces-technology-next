## Study focus
A short early study connecting Modular Open Systems Approach principles to experimental microcontroller code organization and communications.

The original note discusses separated sensor and network source modules, defined external interfaces, structured JSON messages, and verification of observable interface behavior.

## Interpretation
MOSA is a design approach, not certification obtained merely by using modular code. The note is retained as evidence of research direction rather than as a validated standards implementation.
