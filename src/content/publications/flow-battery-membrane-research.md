## Research context
The undergraduate symposium poster examined methods for determining chemical stability of ion-exchange membranes used in redox flow battery applications.

## Methods and reported findings
The team investigated open-circuit voltage and cyclic voltammetry using three-electrode arrangements and different electrode and bridge materials. Reported OCV data were consistent with the Nernst equation under the tested setup, while cyclic-voltammetry results in nonstandard setups remained inconclusive.
