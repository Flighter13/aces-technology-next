## Project brief

NexGen Air Battlespace is an interactive research visualization of aircraft and supporting systems, mission concepts, and relationships in modern and emerging air operations. It combines a geographic scenario view, an interactive network view, and supporting evidence and aircraft profiles.

**[Launch the interactive NexGen Air Battlespace research atlas](https://flighter13.github.io/NexGen-Air-Battlespace/research/)**

[View the NexGen source repository](https://github.com/Flighter13/NexGen-Air-Battlespace).

## Research features

- Interactive geographic view with aircraft silhouettes, adjustable playback, flight-heading orientation, and selectable participants.
- Network view showing aircraft, support systems, and their documented or analytical relationships.
- Mission phase timelines and aircraft details with evidence and source references.
- Research scenarios informed by public Mitchell Institute collaborative combat aircraft tabletop-exercise reporting.

## Scope and limitations

The research atlas is **not an operational planning system or validated combat simulation**. Published tabletop-exercise concepts, actor roles, and some quantities can be source-backed, while illustrated coordinates, aircraft spacing, simulated motion, and unreported formations must be understood as explanatory reconstructions.

The aircraft and CCA catalog includes real-world aircraft as well as **notional** exercise variants. Generic and notional icons do not assert a verified physical aircraft design.

## Related engineering work

NexGen complements [the PURiK Modular Sensor System](/projects/purik-sensor-system/): NexGen examines systems-of-systems relationships in an interactive research environment, while PURiK explores modular sensing, physical interfaces, and integration evidence with experimental hardware.
